# Scrapbook scraps

These are 33 torn-paper pieces cut on 2026-09-23 from Codex's eight OpenAI `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` collage plates, which are the same plates behind `films/momo-collage`. All the imagery is AI generated: no real people, no client material, no third-party marks.

**How they were cut:**

1. Cream and white torn edges (bright, low saturation) mark where one piece ends.
2. Each photo region between them is filled.
3. The region is grown 6px so it keeps a sliver of its own torn edge.
4. Its alpha is feathered by 1.2px.
5. Each piece is scaled to 240px on its long side and saved as WebP with alpha (quality 82), about 330 KB for the set.

**Recipe:** `cut_plates.py` in the session job folder.

**Source plates:** `Documents/Codex/2026-09-23/we-ll-see-how-good-you/work/momo-collage/plates/`.

**Names** say what's in each piece: `compass`, `vase`, `twine`, `headphones`, `handoff`, `storefront`, `brushes`, `exclamation`, `phone`, `call`, `speech-bubbles`, `skyline-meeting`, `sky`, `tablet`, `leaf-shadow`, `cyclist`, `writing`, `coast`, `coast-road`, `circles`, `train`, `handshake`, `night-office`, `laptop`, `seedling`, `santorini`, `old-town` and `sunflowers`. Plain paper scraps are `blue-scrap`, `yellow-scrap`, `blue-strip`, `blue-yellow` and `blue-circle`.
