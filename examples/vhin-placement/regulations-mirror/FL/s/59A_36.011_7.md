# Rule 59A-36.011(7), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.011 Staff Training Requirements and Competency Test
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.011

[Rule 59A-36.011(7), F.A.C.] NUTRITION AND FOOD SERVICE. The administrator or person designated by the administrator as responsible for the facility’s food service and the day-to-day supervision of food service staff must obtain, annually, a minimum of 2 hours continuing education in topics pertinent to nutrition and food service in an assisted living facility. This requirement does not apply to administrators and designees who are exempt from training requirements under paragraph 59A-36.012(1)(b), F.A.C. A certified food manager, licensed dietician, registered dietary technician or health department sanitarian is qualified to train assisted living facility staff in nutrition and food service.
