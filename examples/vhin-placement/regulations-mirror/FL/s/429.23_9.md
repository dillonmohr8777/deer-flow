# Section 429.23(9), F.S.
Section: 429.23 Internal risk management and quality assurance program; adverse incidents and reporting requirements
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.23.html

[Section 429.23(9), F.S.] The adverse incident reports and preliminary adverse incident reports required under this section are confidential as provided by law and are not discoverable or admissible in any civil or administrative action, except in disciplinary proceedings by the agency or appropriate regulatory board.
