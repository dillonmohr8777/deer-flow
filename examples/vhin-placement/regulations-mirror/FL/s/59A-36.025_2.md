# Rule 59A-36.025(2), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.025 Emergency Environmental Control for Assisted Living Facilities
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.025

[Rule 59A-36.025(2), F.A.C.] SUBMISSION OF THE PLAN.
[Rule 59A-36.025(2)(a), F.A.C.] Each new assisted living facility shall submit the plan required under this rule prior to obtaining a license.
[Rule 59A-36.025(2)(b), F.A.C.] Each existing assisted living facility that undergoes any additions, modifications, alterations, refurbishment, renovations or reconstruction that require modification of its systems or equipment affecting the facility’s compliance with this rule shall amend its plan and submit it to the county emergency management agency for review and approval.
