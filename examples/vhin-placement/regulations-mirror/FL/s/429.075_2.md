# Section 429.075(2), F.S.
Section: 429.075 Limited mental health license
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.075.html

[Section 429.075(2), F.S.] A facility that is licensed to provide services to mental health residents must provide appropriate supervision and staffing to provide for the health, safety, and welfare of such residents.
