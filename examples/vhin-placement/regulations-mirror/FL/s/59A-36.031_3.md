# Rule 59A-36.031(3), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.031 Registered ALF Core Training Provider Responsibilities
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.031

[Rule 59A-36.031(3), F.A.C.] RECORDS. Approved providers must maintain records of each course taught for a minimum of 5 years. Course records must include the following information:
[Rule 59A-36.031(3)(a), F.A.C.] The title of the training program;
[Rule 59A-36.031(3)(b), F.A.C.] The agenda;
[Rule 59A-36.031(3)(c), F.A.C.] The curriculum and any accompanying documentation and training aids;
[Rule 59A-36.031(3)(d), F.A.C.] The training provider’s name and registration number;
[Rule 59A-36.031(3)(e), F.A.C.] The trainees’ names, dates of participation and training location; and
[Rule 59A-36.031(3)(f), F.A.C.] Training evaluations and roster signed by trainees.
