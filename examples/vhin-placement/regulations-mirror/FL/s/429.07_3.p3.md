[Section 429.07(3)(b)4., F.S.] A facility that is licensed to provide extended congregate care services must:
[Section 429.07(3)(b)4.a., F.S.] Demonstrate the capability to meet unanticipated resident service needs.
[Section 429.07(3)(b)4.b., F.S.] Offer a physical environment that promotes a homelike setting, provides for resident privacy, promotes resident independence, and allows sufficient congregate space as defined by rule.
[Section 429.07(3)(b)4.c., F.S.] Have sufficient staff available, taking into account the physical plant and firesafety features of the building, to assist with the evacuation of residents in an emergency.
[Section 429.07(3)(b)4.d., F.S.] Adopt and follow policies and procedures that maximize resident independence, dignity, choice, and decisionmaking to permit residents to age in place, so that moves due to changes in functional status are minimized or avoided.
[Section 429.07(3)(b)4.e., F.S.] Allow residents or, if applicable, a resident’s representative, designee, surrogate, guardian, or attorney in fact to make a variety of personal choices, participate in developing service plans, and share responsibility in decisionmaking.
[Section 429.07(3)(b)4.f., F.S.] Implement the concept of managed risk.
[Section 429.07(3)(b)4.g., F.S.] Provide, directly or through contract, the services of a person licensed under part I of chapter 464.
[Section 429.07(3)(b)4.h., F.S.] In addition to the training mandated in s. 429.52, provide specialized training as defined by rule for facility staff.
[Section 429.07(3)(b)5., F.S.] A facility that is licensed to provide extended congregate care services is exempt from the criteria for continued residency set forth in rules adopted under s. 429.41. A licensed facility must adopt its own requirements within guidelines for continued residency set forth by rule. However, the facility may not serve residents who require 24-hour nursing supervision. A licensed facility that provides extended congregate care services must also provide each resident with a written copy of facility policies governing admission and retention.
[Section 429.07(3)(b)6., F.S.] Before the admission of an individual to a facility licensed to provide extended congregate care services, the individual must undergo a medical examination as provided in s. 429.26(5) and the facility must develop a preliminary service plan for the individual.
[Section 429.07(3)(b)7., F.S.] If a facility can no longer provide or arrange for services in accordance with the resident’s service plan and needs and the facility’s policy, the facility must make arrangements for relocating the person in accordance with s. 429.28(1)(k).
[Section 429.07(3)(c), F.S.] A limited nursing services license shall be issued to a facility that provides services beyond those authorized in paragraph (a) and as specified in this paragraph.
