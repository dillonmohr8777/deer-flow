# Section 429.23(4), F.S.
Section: 429.23 Internal risk management and quality assurance program; adverse incidents and reporting requirements
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.23.html

[Section 429.23(4), F.S.] Licensed facilities shall provide within 15 days, through the agency’s online portal, or if the portal is offline, by electronic mail, a full report to the agency on all adverse incidents specified in this section. The report must include the results of the facility’s investigation into the adverse incident.
