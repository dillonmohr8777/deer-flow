# Rule 59A-36.004(1), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.004 License Requirements
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.004

[Rule 59A-36.004(1), F.A.C.] SERVICE PROHIBITION. An assisted living facility may not represent that it provides any service other than a service for which it is licensed to provide.
