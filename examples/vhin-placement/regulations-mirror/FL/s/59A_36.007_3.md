# Rule 59A-36.007(3), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.007 Resident Care Standards
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.007

[Rule 59A-36.007(3), F.A.C.] ARRANGEMENT FOR HEALTH CARE. In order to facilitate resident access to health care as needed, the facility must:
[Rule 59A-36.007(3)(a), F.A.C.] Assist residents in making appointments and remind residents about scheduled appointments for medical, dental, nursing, or mental health services.
[Rule 59A-36.007(3)(b), F.A.C.] Provide transportation to needed medical, dental, nursing or mental health services, or arrange for transportation through family and friends, volunteers, taxi cabs, public buses, and agencies providing transportation.
[Rule 59A-36.007(3)(c), F.A.C.] The facility may not require residents to receive services from a particular health care provider.
