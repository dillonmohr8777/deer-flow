# Section 429.19(6), F.S.
Section: 429.19 Violations; imposition of administrative fines; grounds
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.19.html

[Section 429.19(6), F.S.] Any facility whose owner fails to apply for a change-of-ownership license in accordance with part II of chapter 408 and operates the facility under the new ownership is subject to a fine of $5,000.
