# Rule 59A-36.002(13), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.002 Definitions
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.002

[Rule 59A-36.002(13), F.A.C.] “Direct Care Staff” means Staff in Regular Contact or Staff in Direct Contact with residents who provide personal or nursing services to residents, including administrators and managers providing such services.
