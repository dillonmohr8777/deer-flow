# Section 429.27(8), F.S.
Section: 429.27 Property and personal affairs of residents
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.27.html

[Section 429.27(8), F.S.] The agency may by rule clarify terms and specify procedures and documentation necessary to administer the provisions of this section relating to the proper management of residents’ funds and personal property and the execution of surety bonds.
