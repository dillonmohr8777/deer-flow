# Rule 59A-36.005(1), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.005 Inspection Responsibilities
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.005

[Rule 59A-36.005(1), F.A.C.] County health departments are responsible for inspecting all license applicants and licensed facilities in matters regulated by:
[Rule 59A-36.005(1)(a), F.A.C.] Rule 64E-12.004, F.A.C., and rule Chapter 64E-11, F.A.C., relating to food hygiene.
[Rule 59A-36.005(1)(b), F.A.C.] Chapter 64E-12, F.A.C., relating to sanitary practices in community-based residential facilities.
[Rule 59A-36.005(1)(c), F.A.C.] Chapter 64E-16, F.A.C., relating to biomedical waste.
