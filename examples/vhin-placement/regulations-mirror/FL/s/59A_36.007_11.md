# Rule 59A-36.007(11), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.007 Resident Care Standards
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.007

[Rule 59A-36.007(11), F.A.C.] OTHER STANDARDS. Additional care standards for residents residing in a facility holding a limited mental health, extended congregate care or limited nursing services license are provided in Rules 59A-36.020, 59A-36.021 and 59A-36.022, F.A.C., respectively.
