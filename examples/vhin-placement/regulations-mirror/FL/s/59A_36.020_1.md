# Rule 59A-36.020(1), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.020 Limited Mental Health
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.020

[Rule 59A-36.020(1), F.A.C.] LICENSE APPLICATION.
[Rule 59A-36.020(1)(a), F.A.C.] Any facility intending to admit one or more mental health residents must obtain a limited mental health license from the agency before accepting the mental health resident.
[Rule 59A-36.020(1)(b), F.A.C.] Facilities applying for a limited mental health license that have uncorrected deficiencies or violations found during the facility’s last survey, complaint investigation, or monitoring visit will be surveyed before the issuance of a limited mental health license to determine if such deficiencies or violations have been corrected.
