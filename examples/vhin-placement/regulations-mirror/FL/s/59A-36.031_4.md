# Rule 59A-36.031(4), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.031 Registered ALF Core Training Provider Responsibilities
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.031

[Rule 59A-36.031(4), F.A.C.] COMPETENCY EXAM.
[Rule 59A-36.031(4)(a), F.A.C.] Approved training providers must submit the names of trainees completing core training to the testing authority within 10 calendar days after completion of the course. Names must be submitted to the following address: ALF at The MacDonald Research Institute, 28953 SR 54, Wesley Chapel, FL 33543. Names may be alternately submitted via email to alftest.tmri@gmail.com.
[Rule 59A-36.031(4)(b), F.A.C.] The testing authority shall not process any requests for the competency exam, nor sit any individual for the exam, unless proper notice is submitted by an approved training provider pursuant to paragraph (4)(a) of this subsection.
