# Rule 59A-36.014(1), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.014 Physical Plant Standards
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.014

[Rule 59A-36.014(1), F.A.C.] NEW FACILITIES. Newly constructed facilities to be licensed as assisted living facilities, and existing structures, not previously licensed as assisted living facilities, to be converted to assisted living facilities, as well as any subsequent additions, modifications, alterations, renovations or refurbishing of such facilities, are required by governmental entities other than the Department of Elder Affairs to adhere to certain building code and fire safety standards. Such standards may be found in:
[Rule 59A-36.014(1)(a), F.A.C.] Chapter 4, Section 464, of the Florida Building Code as adopted in Rule 61G20-1.001, F.A.C.;
[Rule 59A-36.014(1)(b), F.A.C.] Section 633.022, F.S., Uniform Firesafety Standards and rule Chapter 69A-40, F.A.C. The Uniform Fire Safety Standards for Assisted Living Facilities; and,
[Rule 59A-36.014(1)(c), F.A.C.] The National Fire Protection Association codes described in Section 429.41, F.S.
