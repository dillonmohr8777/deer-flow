# Section 429.22(10), F.S.
Section: 429.22 Receivership proceedings
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.22.html

[Section 429.22(10), F.S.] The court may terminate a receivership when:
[Section 429.22(10)(a), F.S.] The court determines that the receivership is no longer necessary because the conditions which gave rise to the receivership no longer exist or the agency grants the facility a new license; or
[Section 429.22(10)(b), F.S.] All of the residents in the facility have been transferred or discharged.
