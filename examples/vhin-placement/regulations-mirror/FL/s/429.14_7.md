# Section 429.14(7), F.S.
Section: 429.14 Administrative penalties
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.14.html

[Section 429.14(7), F.S.] Agency notification of a license suspension or revocation, or denial of a license renewal, shall be posted and visible to the public at the facility.
