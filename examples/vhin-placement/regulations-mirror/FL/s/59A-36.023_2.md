# Rule 59A-36.023(2), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.023 Administrative Enforcement
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.023

[Rule 59A-36.023(2), F.A.C.] Survey Deficiency.
[Rule 59A-36.023(2)(a), F.A.C.] Before or in conjunction with a notice of violation issued pursuant to Part II, Chapter 408, F.S., and Section 429.19, F.S., the agency shall issue a statement of deficiency for class I, II, III, and IV violations which are observed by agency personnel during any inspection of the facility. The deficiency statement must be issued within 10 working days of the agency’s inspection and must include:
[Rule 59A-36.023(2)(a)1., F.A.C.] A description of the deficiency;
[Rule 59A-36.023(2)(a)2., F.A.C.] A citation to the statute or rule violated; and
[Rule 59A-36.023(2)(a)3., F.A.C.] A time frame for the correction of the deficiency.
[Rule 59A-36.023(2)(b), F.A.C.] Additional time may be granted to correct specific deficiencies if a written request is received by the agency before the expiration of the time frame included in the agency’s statement.
