# Rule 59A-36.002(26), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.002 Definitions
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.002

[Rule 59A-36.002(26), F.A.C.] “Nurse” means a licensed practical nurse (LPN), registered nurse (RN), or advanced practice registered nurse (APRN) licensed under Chapter 464, F.S.
