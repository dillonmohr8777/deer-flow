# Rule 59A-36.028(3), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.028 ALF Minimum Core Training Curriculum Requirements
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.028

[Rule 59A-36.028(3), F.A.C.] CANCELLATION OF CORE REGISTRATION. The Agency may cancel the core registration and remove any ALF core training provider from the approved list who fails to:
[Rule 59A-36.028(3)(a), F.A.C.] Submit a core training schedule within 10 business days after the request;
[Rule 59A-36.028(3)(b), F.A.C.] Comply with a corrective action plan issued by the Agency within 45 calendar days after the notification;
[Rule 59A-36.028(3)(c), F.A.C.] Have a student passage rate at or above 55% as determined by the Agency’s contracted vendor in the preceding calendar year;
[Rule 59A-36.028(3)(d), F.A.C.] Conduct an ALF core training session in the preceding calendar year; or
[Rule 59A-36.028(3)(e), F.A.C.] Submit at least 10 examinees’ names to the Agency’s contracted ALF core training testing vendor in the preceding calendar year.
