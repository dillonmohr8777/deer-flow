# Rule 59A-36.011(3), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.011 Staff Training Requirements and Competency Test
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.011

[Rule 59A-36.011(3), F.A.C.] STAFF IN-SERVICE TRAINING. Facility administrators or managers shall provide or arrange for the following in-service training to facility staff:
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(a), F.A.C.] Staff who provide direct care to residents, other than nurses, certified nursing assistants, or home health aides trained in accordance with Rule 59A-8.0095, F.A.C., must receive a minimum of 1 hour in-service training in infection control, including universal precautions and facility sanitation procedures, before providing personal care to residents. The facility must use its infection control policies and procedures when offering this training. Documentation of compliance with the staff training requirements of 29 CFR 1910.1030, relating to blood borne pathogens, may be used to meet this requirement.
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(b), F.A.C.] Staff who provide direct care to residents must receive a minimum of 1 hour in-service training within 30 days of employment that covers the following subjects:
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(b)1., F.A.C.] Reporting adverse incidents.
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(b)2., F.A.C.] Facility emergency procedures including chain-of-command and staff roles relating to emergency evacuation.
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(c), F.A.C.] Staff who provide direct care to residents, who have not taken the core training program, shall receive a minimum of 1 hour in-service training within 30 days of employment that covers the following subjects:
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(c)1., F.A.C.] Resident rights in an assisted living facility.
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(c)2., F.A.C.] Recognizing and reporting resident abuse, neglect, and exploitation. The facility must use its abuse prevention policies and procedures when offering this training.
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(d), F.A.C.] Staff who provide direct care to residents, other than nurses, CNAs, or home health aides trained in accordance with Rule 59A-8.0095, F.A.C., must receive 3 hours of in-service training within 30 days of employment that covers the following subjects:
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(d)1., F.A.C.] Resident behavior and needs.
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(d)2., F.A.C.] Providing assistance with the activities of daily living.
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(e), F.A.C.] Staff who prepare or serve food, who have not taken the assisted living facility core training must receive a minimum of 1-hour-in-service training within 30 days of employment in safe food handling practices.
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(f), F.A.C.] All facility staff shall receive in-service training regarding the facility’s resident elopement response policies and procedures within thirty (30) days of employment.
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(f)1., F.A.C.] All facility staff shall be provided with a copy of the facility’s resident elopement response policies and procedures.
[Rule 59A-36.011(3)(f)2., F.A.C.] All facility staff shall demonstrate an understanding and competency in the implementation of the elopement response policies and procedures.
