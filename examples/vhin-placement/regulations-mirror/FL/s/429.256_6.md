# Section 429.256(6), F.S.
Section: 429.256 Assistance with self-administration of medication and with other tasks
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.256.html

[Section 429.256(6), F.S.] Assistance with other tasks includes:
[Section 429.256(6)(a), F.S.] Assisting with the use of a glucometer to perform blood-glucose level checks.
[Section 429.256(6)(b), F.S.] Assisting with putting on and taking off antiembolism stockings.
[Section 429.256(6)(c), F.S.] Assisting with applying and removing an oxygen cannula but not with titrating the prescribed oxygen settings.
[Section 429.256(6)(d), F.S.] Assisting with the use of a continuous positive airway pressure device but not with titrating the prescribed setting of the device.
[Section 429.256(6)(e), F.S.] Assisting with measuring vital signs.
[Section 429.256(6)(f), F.S.] Assisting with colostomy bags.
