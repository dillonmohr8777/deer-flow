# Rule 59A-36.002(18), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.002 Definitions
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.002

[Rule 59A-36.002(18), F.A.C.] “Health Care Provider” means a health care practitioner licensed under Chapter 458, 459, or 464, F.S., as a physician, physician assistant, or advanced practice registered nurse; or an individual, entity, or organization licensed by the state to provide health care services.
