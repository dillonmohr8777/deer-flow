# Rule 59A-36.002(4), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.002 Definitions
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.002

[Rule 59A-36.002(4), F.A.C.] “Apartment” means a self-contained dwelling unit with a bathroom, kitchen area, and living and sleeping space that is contracted for use as a residence by one or more persons who maintain a common household.
