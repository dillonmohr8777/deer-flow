# Section 429.24(4), F.S.
Section: 429.24 Contracts
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.24.html

[Section 429.24(4), F.S.] The contract shall state whether or not the facility is affiliated with any religious organization and, if so, which organization and its general responsibility to the facility.
