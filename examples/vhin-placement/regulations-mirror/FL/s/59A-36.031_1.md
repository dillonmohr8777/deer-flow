# Rule 59A-36.031(1), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.031 Registered ALF Core Training Provider Responsibilities
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.031

[Rule 59A-36.031(1), F.A.C.] USE OF MINIMUM CORE TRAINING CURRICULUM. A registered core training provider is responsible for the following:
[Rule 59A-36.031(1)(a), F.A.C.] Conducting core training using the minimum core training curriculum required by Rule 59A-36.028, F.A.C.; and
[Rule 59A-36.031(1)(b), F.A.C.] Ensuring that all changes in ALF statutes and rules are immediately incorporated into the contents of his or her core training curriculum.
