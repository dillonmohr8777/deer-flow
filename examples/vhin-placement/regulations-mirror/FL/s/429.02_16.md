# Section 429.02(16), F.S.
Section: 429.02 Definitions
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.02.html

[Section 429.02(16), F.S.] “Memory care resident” means a person who suffers from Alzheimer’s disease or a related dementia who is a resident of an assisted living facility that claims or otherwise represents that it provides specialized care, services, or activities specifically to support such resident’s Alzheimer’s disease or related dementia, irrespective of whether such care, services, or activities were listed in the resident’s contract.
