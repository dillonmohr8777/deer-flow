# Section 429.076(5)(a), F.S.
Section: 429.076 Memory care services license
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.076.html

[Section 429.076(5)(a), F.S.] A facility that serves one or more memory care residents accepted before the effective date of the rules required by subsection (2) may continue to serve such memory care residents and provide memory care services to such residents without obtaining a memory care services license if the facility:
[Section 429.076(5)(a)1., F.S.] Demonstrates to the agency that it is unable to reasonably obtain such license.
[Section 429.076(5)(a)2., F.S.] Notifies any memory care residents the facility serves and their caregivers, if applicable, that:
[Section 429.076(5)(a)2.a., F.S.] The facility is required to obtain a memory care services license;
[Section 429.076(5)(a)2.b., F.S.] The facility is unable to obtain such license; and
[Section 429.076(5)(a)2.c., F.S.] The memory care resident may relocate to a facility with a memory care services license, if desired.
[Section 429.076(5)(a)3., F.S.] Upon request, assists memory care residents or, if applicable, their caregivers with finding a suitable alternate facility.
[Section 429.076(5)(a)4., F.S.] No longer accepts any new memory care residents without first obtaining a memory care services license.
[Section 429.076(5)(b), F.S.] If, after receiving the notice required by subparagraph (a)2., a memory care resident or, if applicable, his or her caregiver decides that the resident will remain at the facility, the facility must:
[Section 429.076(5)(b)1., F.S.] Amend the resident’s contract to include the memory care services that are being provided to the resident;
[Section 429.076(5)(b)2., F.S.] Maintain records pertaining to when and how such services were provided to the resident; and
[Section 429.076(5)(b)3., F.S.] Provide such records to the resident, his or her caregivers, or the agency upon request.
[Section 429.076(5)(c), F.S.] This subsection may not be construed to exempt a facility from meeting any other requirements in law or rule as applicable to the facility, including, but not limited to, requirements related to the appropriateness of placements for residents of the facility established in s. 429.26.
