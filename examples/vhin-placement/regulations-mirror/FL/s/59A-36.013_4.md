# Rule 59A-36.013(4), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.013 Fiscal Standards
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.013

[Rule 59A-36.013(4), F.A.C.] LIABILITY INSURANCE. Pursuant to Section 429.275, F.S., facilities must maintain liability insurance coverage, as defined in Section 624.605, F.S., that remains in force at all times. On the renewal date of the facility’s policy or whenever a facility changes policies, the facility must file documentation of continued coverage with the Agency Central Office. Such documentation must be issued by the insurance company and must include the name and street address of the facility, a reference that the facility is an assisted living facility, the facility’s licensed capacity, and the dates of coverage.
