# Section 429.53(2), F.S.
Section: 429.53 Consultation by the agency
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.53.html

[Section 429.53(2), F.S.] As used in this section, “consultation” includes:
[Section 429.53(2)(a), F.S.] An explanation of the requirements of this part and rules adopted pursuant thereto;
[Section 429.53(2)(b), F.S.] An explanation of the license application and renewal procedures;
[Section 429.53(2)(c), F.S.] The provision of a checklist of general local and state approvals required prior to constructing or developing a facility and a listing of the types of agencies responsible for such approvals;
[Section 429.53(2)(d), F.S.] An explanation of benefits and financial assistance available to a recipient of supplemental security income residing in a facility;
[Section 429.53(2)(e), F.S.] Any other information which the agency deems necessary to promote compliance with the requirements of this part; and
[Section 429.53(2)(f), F.S.] A preconstruction review of a facility to ensure compliance with agency rules and this part.
