# Section 429.52(11), F.S.
Section: 429.52 Staff training and educational requirements
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.52.html

[Section 429.52(11), F.S.] A person seeking to register as a core trainer also must:
[Section 429.52(11)(a), F.S.] Provide proof of completion of a 4-year degree from an accredited college or university and must have worked in a management position in an assisted living facility for 3 years after being core certified;
[Section 429.52(11)(b), F.S.] Have worked in a management position in an assisted living facility for 5 years after being core certified and have 1 year of teaching experience as an educator or staff trainer for persons who work in assisted living facilities or other long-term care settings;
[Section 429.52(11)(c), F.S.] Have been previously employed as a core trainer for the agency or department; or
[Section 429.52(11)(d), F.S.] Meet other qualification criteria as defined in rule, which the agency is authorized to adopt.
