# Rule 59A-36.004(6), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.004 License Requirements
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.004

[Rule 59A-36.004(6), F.A.C.] RESIDENTS RECEIVING STATE-FUNDED SERVICES. Upon request, the facility administrator or designee must identify residents receiving state-funded services to the agency and the department for monitoring purposes authorized by state and federal laws.
