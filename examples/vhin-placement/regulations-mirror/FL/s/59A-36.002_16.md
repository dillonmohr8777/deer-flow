# Rule 59A-36.002(16), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.002 Definitions
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.002

[Rule 59A-36.002(16), F.A.C.] “Food Service” means the storage, preparation, service, and clean up of food intended for consumption in a facility either by facility staff or through a formal agreement that meals will be regularly catered by a third party.
