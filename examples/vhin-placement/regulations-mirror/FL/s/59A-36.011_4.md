# Rule 59A-36.011(4), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.011 Staff Training Requirements and Competency Test
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.011

[Rule 59A-36.011(4), F.A.C.] HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS/ACQUIRED IMMUNE DEFICIENCY SYNDROME (HIV/AIDS). Pursuant to Section 381.0035, F.S., all facility employees, with the exception of employees subject to the requirements of Section 456.033, F.S., must complete a one-time education course on HIV and AIDS, including the topics prescribed in the Section 381.0035, F.S. New facility staff must obtain the training within 30 days of employment. Documentation of compliance must be maintained in accordance with subsection (12) of this rule.
