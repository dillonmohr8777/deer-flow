# Section 429.41(3), F.S.
Section: 429.41 Rules establishing standards
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.41.html

[Section 429.41(3), F.S.] Rules adopted by the agency shall encourage the development of homelike facilities that promote the dignity, individuality, personal strengths, and decisionmaking ability of residents.
