# Rule 59A-36.005(3), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.005 Inspection Responsibilities
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.005

[Rule 59A-36.005(3), F.A.C.] The agency is responsible for inspecting all license applicants and licensed facilities in all other matters regulated by this rule chapter.
