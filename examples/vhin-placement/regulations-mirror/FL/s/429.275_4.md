# Section 429.275(4), F.S.
Section: 429.275 Business practice; personnel records; liability insurance
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.275.html

[Section 429.275(4), F.S.] The agency may by rule clarify terms, establish requirements for financial records, accounting procedures, personnel procedures, insurance coverage, and reporting procedures, and specify documentation as necessary to implement this section.
