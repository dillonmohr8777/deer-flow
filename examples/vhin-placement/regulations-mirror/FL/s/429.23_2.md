# Section 429.23(2), F.S.
Section: 429.23 Internal risk management and quality assurance program; adverse incidents and reporting requirements
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.23.html

[Section 429.23(2), F.S.] Every facility licensed under this part is required to maintain adverse incident reports. For purposes of this section, the term, “adverse incident” means:
[Section 429.23(2)(a), F.S.] An event over which facility personnel could exercise control rather than as a result of the resident’s condition and results in:
[Section 429.23(2)(a)1., F.S.] Death;
[Section 429.23(2)(a)2., F.S.] Brain or spinal damage;
[Section 429.23(2)(a)3., F.S.] Permanent disfigurement;
[Section 429.23(2)(a)4., F.S.] Fracture or dislocation of bones or joints;
[Section 429.23(2)(a)5., F.S.] Any condition that required medical attention to which the resident has not given his or her consent, including failure to honor advanced directives;
[Section 429.23(2)(a)6., F.S.] Any condition that requires the transfer of the resident from the facility to a unit providing more acute care due to the incident rather than the resident’s condition before the incident; or
[Section 429.23(2)(a)7., F.S.] An event that is reported to law enforcement or its personnel for investigation; or
[Section 429.23(2)(b), F.S.] Resident elopement, if the elopement places the resident at risk of harm or injury.
