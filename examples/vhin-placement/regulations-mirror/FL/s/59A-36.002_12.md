# Rule 59A-36.002(12), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.002 Definitions
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.002

[Rule 59A-36.002(12), F.A.C.] “Deficiency” means an instance of non-compliance with the requirements of Part II of Chapter 408, F.S., Part I of Chapter 429, F.S., rule Chapter 59A-35, F.A.C., and this rule chapter.
