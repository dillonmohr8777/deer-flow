# Rule 59A-36.021(1), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.021 Extended Congregate Care Services
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.021

[Rule 59A-36.021(1), F.A.C.] LICENSING.
[Rule 59A-36.021(1)(a), F.A.C.] Any facility intending to establish extended congregate care services must obtain a license from the agency before accepting residents needing extended congregate care services.
[Rule 59A-36.021(1)(b), F.A.C.] Only the portion of a facility that meets the physical requirements for extended congregate care in Chapter 4, Section 464 of the Florida Building Code as adopted in Rule 61G20-1.001, F.A.C., and is staffed in accordance with subsection (3), is considered licensed to provide extended congregate care services to residents who meet the admission and continued residency requirements of this rule.
