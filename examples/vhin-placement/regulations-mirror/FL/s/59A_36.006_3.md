# Rule 59A-36.006(3), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.006 Admission Procedures, Appropriateness of Placement and Continued Residency Criteria
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.006

[Rule 59A-36.006(3), F.A.C.] ADMISSION PACKAGE.
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a), F.A.C.] The facility must make available to potential residents a written statement(s) that includes the following information listed below. Providing a copy of the facility resident contract or facility brochure containing all the required information meets this requirement.
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)1., F.A.C.] The facility’s admission and continued residency criteria;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)2., F.A.C.] The daily, weekly or monthly charge to reside in the facility and the services, supplies, and accommodations provided by the facility for that rate;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)3., F.A.C.] Personal care services that the facility is prepared to provide to residents and additional costs to the resident, if any;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)4., F.A.C.] Nursing services that the facility is prepared to provide to residents and additional costs to the resident, if any;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)5., F.A.C.] Food service and the ability of the facility to accommodate special diets;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)6., F.A.C.] The availability of transportation and additional costs to the resident, if any;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)7., F.A.C.] Any other special services that are provided by the facility and additional cost if any;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)8., F.A.C.] Social and leisure activities generally offered by the facility;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)9., F.A.C.] Any services that the facility does not provide but will arrange for the resident and additional cost, if any;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)10., F.A.C.] The facility rules and regulations that residents must follow as described in Rule 59A-36.007, F.A.C.;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)11., F.A.C.] The facility policy concerning Do Not Resuscitate Orders pursuant to Section 429.255, F.S., and Rule 59A-36.009, F.A.C., and Advance Directives pursuant to Chapter 765, F.S.;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)12., F.A.C.] If the facility is licensed to provide extended congregate care, the facility’s residency criteria for residents receiving extended congregate care services. The facility must also provide a description of the additional personal, supportive, and nursing services provided by the facility including additional costs and any limitations on where extended congregate care residents may reside based on the policies and procedures described in Rule 59A-36.021, F.A.C.;
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)13., F.A.C.] If the facility advertises that it provides special care for individuals with Alzheimer’s disease and related disorders, a written description of those special services as required in Section 429.177, F.S.; and,
[Rule 59A-36.006(3)(a)14., F.A.C.] The facility’s resident elopement response policies and procedures.
