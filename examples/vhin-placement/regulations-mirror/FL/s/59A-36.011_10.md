# Rule 59A-36.011(10), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.011 Staff Training Requirements and Competency Test
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.011

[Rule 59A-36.011(10), F.A.C.] ALZHEIMER’S DISEASE AND RELATED DISORDERS (“ADRD”) TRAINING REQUIREMENTS. Facilities which advertise that they provide special care for persons with ADRD, or who maintain secured areas as described in Chapter 4, Section 464.4.6 of the Florida Building Code, as adopted in Rule 61G20-1.001, F.A.C., Florida Building Code Adopted, must ensure that facility staff receive training in accordance with Section 430.5025, Florida Statutes.
