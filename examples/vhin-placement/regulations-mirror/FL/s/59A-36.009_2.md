# Rule 59A-36.009(2), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.009 Do Not Resuscitate Orders (DNROs) and Advance Directives
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.009

[Rule 59A-36.009(2), F.A.C.] LICENSE REVOCATION. An assisted living facility’s license is subject to revocation pursuant to Section 408.815, F.S., if, as a condition of treatment or admission, the facility requires an individual to execute or waive DH Form 1896.
