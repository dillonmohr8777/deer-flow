# Rule 59A-36.012(4), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.012 Food Service Standards
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.012

[Rule 59A-36.012(4), F.A.C.] CONTRACTED FOOD SERVICE. When food service is contracted by the facility, the facility must ensure that the contracted food service meets all dietary standards imposed by this rule and is adequately protected upon delivery to the facility pursuant to subsection 64E-12.004(4), F.A.C. The facility must maintain:
[Rule 59A-36.012(4)(a), F.A.C.] A copy of the current contract between the facility and the food service contractor.
[Rule 59A-36.012(4)(b), F.A.C.] A copy of the annually issued certificate or license authorizing the operation of the food service contractor issued by the applicable regulating agency. The license or certificate must provide documentation of the food service contractor’s compliance with food service regulatory requirements.
