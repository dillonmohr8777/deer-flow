# Section 429.24(5), F.S.
Section: 429.24 Contracts
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.24.html

[Section 429.24(5), F.S.] Neither the contract nor any provision thereof relieves any licensee of any requirement or obligation imposed upon it by this part or rules adopted under this part.
