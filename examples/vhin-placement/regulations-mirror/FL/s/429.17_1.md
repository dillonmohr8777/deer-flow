# Section 429.17(1), F.S.
Section: 429.17 Expiration of license; renewal; conditional license
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.17.html

[Section 429.17(1), F.S.] Limited nursing, extended congregate care, limited mental health licenses, and memory care services shall expire at the same time as the facility’s standard license, regardless of when issued.
