# Rule 59A-36.025(7), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.025 Emergency Environmental Control for Assisted Living Facilities
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.025

[Rule 59A-36.025(7), F.A.C.] COMPREHENSIVE EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT PLAN.
[Rule 59A-36.025(7)(a), F.A.C.] Assisted living facilities whose comprehensive emergency management plan is to evacuate must comply with this rule.
[Rule 59A-36.025(7)(b), F.A.C.] Each facility whose plan has been approved shall submit the plan as an addendum with any future submissions for approval of its comprehensive emergency management plan.
