# Rule 59A-36.029(2), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.029 ALF Core Training Provider Initial Registration Process
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.029

[Rule 59A-36.029(2), F.A.C.] APPROVAL PROCESS. Within 30 calendar days after receiving a core training provider application, the Agency must submit written notification approving or denying the application, or requesting supplemental information or clarification.
[Rule 59A-36.029(2)(a), F.A.C.] If the application is approved, the Agency must include a unique provider registration number in the notice.
[Rule 59A-36.029(2)(b), F.A.C.] If the application is denied, the Agency must provide the reason or reasons for denial in the notice.
[Rule 59A-36.029(2)(c), F.A.C.] If the application is determined to require supplemental information or clarification, the Agency must state the supplemental information or clarification that is being requested.
[Rule 59A-36.029(2)(c)1., F.A.C.] If the Agency does not receive the requested information within 30 calendar days of the request, the application will be deemed incomplete and closed.
[Rule 59A-36.029(2)(c)2., F.A.C.] If the Agency receives the requested information within 30 calendar days of the request, the Agency must process the training provider application within 30 calendar days after all required information is received.
