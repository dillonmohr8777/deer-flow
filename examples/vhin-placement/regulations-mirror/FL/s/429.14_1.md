# Section 429.14(1), F.S.
Section: 429.14 Administrative penalties
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.14.html

[Section 429.14(1), F.S.] In addition to the requirements of part II of chapter 408, the agency may deny, revoke, and suspend any license issued under this part and impose an administrative fine in the manner provided in chapter 120 against a licensee for a violation of any provision of this part, part II of chapter 408, or applicable rules, or for any of the following actions by a licensee, any person subject to level 2 background screening under s. 408.809, or any facility staff:
[Section 429.14(1)(a), F.S.] An intentional or negligent act seriously affecting the health, safety, or welfare of a resident of the facility.
[Section 429.14(1)(b), F.S.] A determination by the agency that the owner lacks the financial ability to provide continuing adequate care to residents.
[Section 429.14(1)(c), F.S.] Misappropriation or conversion of the property of a resident of the facility.
[Section 429.14(1)(d), F.S.] Failure to follow the criteria and procedures provided under part I of chapter 394 relating to the transportation, voluntary admission, and involuntary examination of a facility resident.
[Section 429.14(1)(e), F.S.] A citation for any of the following violations as specified in s. 429.19:
[Section 429.14(1)(e)1., F.S.] One or more cited class I violations.
[Section 429.14(1)(e)2., F.S.] Three or more cited class II violations.
[Section 429.14(1)(e)3., F.S.] Five or more cited class III violations that have been cited on a single survey and have not been corrected within the times specified.
[Section 429.14(1)(f), F.S.] Failure to comply with the background screening standards of this part, s. 408.809(1), or chapter 435.
[Section 429.14(1)(g), F.S.] Violation of a moratorium.
[Section 429.14(1)(h), F.S.] Failure of the license applicant, the licensee during relicensure, or a licensee that holds a provisional license to meet the minimum license requirements of this part, or related rules, at the time of license application or renewal.
[Section 429.14(1)(i), F.S.] An intentional or negligent life-threatening act in violation of the uniform firesafety standards for assisted living facilities or other firesafety standards which threatens the health, safety, or welfare of a resident of a facility, as communicated to the agency by the local authority having jurisdiction or the State Fire Marshal.
[Section 429.14(1)(j), F.S.] Knowingly operating any unlicensed facility or providing without a license any service that must be licensed under this chapter or chapter 400.
[Section 429.14(1)(k), F.S.] Any act constituting a ground upon which application for a license may be denied.
