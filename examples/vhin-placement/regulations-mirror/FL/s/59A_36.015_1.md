# Rule 59A-36.015(1), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.015 Records
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.015

[Rule 59A-36.015(1), F.A.C.] FACILITY RECORDS. Facility records must include:
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(a), F.A.C.] The facility’s license displayed in a conspicuous and public place within the facility.
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(b), F.A.C.] An up-to-date admission and discharge log listing the names of all residents and each resident’s:
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(b)1., F.A.C.] Date of admission, the facility or place from which the resident was admitted, and if applicable, a notation indicating that the resident was admitted with a stage 2 pressure sore; and,
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(b)2., F.A.C.] Date of discharge, reason for discharge, and identification of the facility or home address to which the resident was discharged. Readmission of a resident to the facility after discharge requires a new entry in the log. Discharge of a resident is not required if the facility is holding a bed for a resident who is out of the facility but intending to return pursuant to Rule 59A-36.018, F.A.C. If the resident dies while in the care of the facility, the log must indicate the date of death.
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(c), F.A.C.] A log listing the names of all temporary emergency placement and respite care residents if not included on the log described in paragraph (b).
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(d), F.A.C.] The facility’s emergency management plan, with documentation of review and approval by the county emergency management agency, as described in Rule 59A-36.019, F.A.C., that must be readily available by facility staff.
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(e), F.A.C.] The facility’s liability insurance policy required in Rule 59A-36.013, F.A.C.
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(f), F.A.C.] For facilities that have a surety bond, a copy of the surety bond currently in effect as required by Rule 59A-36.013, F.A.C.
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(g), F.A.C.] The admission package presented to new or prospective residents (less the resident’s contract) described in Rule 59A-36.006, F.A.C.
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(h), F.A.C.] If the facility advertises that it provides special care for persons with Alzheimer’s disease or related disorders, a copy of all such facility advertisements as required by Section 429.177, F.S.
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(i), F.A.C.] A grievance procedure for receiving and responding to resident complaints and recommendations as described in Rule 59A-36.007, F.A.C.
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(j), F.A.C.] All food service records required in Rule 59A-36.012, F.A.C., including menus planned and served and county health department inspection reports. Facilities that contract for food services, must include a copy of the contract for food services and the food service contractor’s license or certificate to operate.
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(k), F.A.C.] All fire safety inspection reports issued by the local authority or the State Fire Marshal pursuant to Section 429.435, F.S., and rule Chapter 69A-40, F.A.C., issued within the last 2 years.
[Rule 59A-36.015(1)(l), F.A.C.] All sanitation inspection reports issued by the county health department pursuant to Section 381.031, F.S., and Chapter 64E-12, F.A.C., issued within the last 2 years.
