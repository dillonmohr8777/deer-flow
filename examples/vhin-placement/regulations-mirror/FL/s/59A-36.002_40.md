# Rule 59A-36.002(40), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.002 Definitions
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.002

[Rule 59A-36.002(40), F.A.C.] “Unscheduled Service Need” means a need for a personal service, nursing service, or mental health intervention that cannot be predicted in advance and that must be met promptly to ensure that the health, safety, and welfare of residents is preserved.
