# Rule 59A-36.002(27), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.002 Definitions
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.002

[Rule 59A-36.002(27), F.A.C.] “Nursing Assessment” means a written review of information collected from observation and interaction with a resident, including the resident’s record and any other relevant sources of information, the analysis of the information, and recommendations for modification of the resident’s care, if warranted. The assessment must contain the signature and credential initials of the advanced practice registered nurse or registered nurse who conducted the assessment.
