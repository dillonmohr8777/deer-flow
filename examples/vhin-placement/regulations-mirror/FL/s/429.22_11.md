# Section 429.22(11), F.S.
Section: 429.22 Receivership proceedings
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.22.html

[Section 429.22(11), F.S.] Within 30 days after termination, the receiver shall give the court a complete accounting of all property of which the receiver has taken possession, of all funds collected, and of the expenses of the receivership.
