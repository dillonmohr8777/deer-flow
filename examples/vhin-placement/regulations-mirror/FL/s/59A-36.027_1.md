# Rule 59A-36.027(1), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.027 ALF Core Training Provider Qualifications
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.027

[Rule 59A-36.027(1), F.A.C.] PRIMARY REQUIREMENTS. In order to register as an assisted living facility core training provider (hereafter referred to as “core training provider,” “training provider,” or “provider”), all applicants must meet the requirements outlined in Section 429.52(10), F.S. The requirements are as follows:
[Rule 59A-36.027(1)(a), F.A.C.] Completion of the minimum core training requirements developed by the Agency pursuant to Section 429.52(9), F.S., and Rule 59A-36.028, F.A.C.;
[Rule 59A-36.027(1)(b), F.A.C.] Successful passage of the competency test, which requires a minimum score of 75%; and,
[Rule 59A-36.027(1)(c), F.A.C.] Compliance with the minimum of 12 contact hours of continuing education in topics related to assisted living every 2 years pursuant to Section 429.52(5), F.S., and paragraph 59A-36.011(1)(c), F.A.C.
