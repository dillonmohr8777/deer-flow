# Rule 59A-36.013(1), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.013 Fiscal Standards
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.013

[Rule 59A-36.013(1), F.A.C.] FINANCIAL STABILITY. The facility must be administered on a sound financial basis in order to ensure adequate resources to meet resident needs pursuant to the requirements of Chapter 408, Part II, Part I, F.S., and rule Chapter 59A-35, F.A.C., and this rule chapter.
