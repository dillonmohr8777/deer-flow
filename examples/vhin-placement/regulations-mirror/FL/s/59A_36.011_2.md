# Rule 59A-36.011(2), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.011 Staff Training Requirements and Competency Test
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.011

[Rule 59A-36.011(2), F.A.C.] STAFF PRESERVICE ORIENTATION.
[Rule 59A-36.011(2)(a), F.A.C.] Facilities must provide a preservice orientation of at least 2 hours to all new assisted living facility employees who have not previously completed core training as detailed in subsection (1).
[Rule 59A-36.011(2)(b), F.A.C.] New staff must complete the preservice orientation prior to interacting with residents.
[Rule 59A-36.011(2)(c), F.A.C.] Once complete, the employee and the facility administrator must sign a statement that the employee completed the preservice orientation which must be kept in the employee’s personnel record.
[Rule 59A-36.011(2)(d), F.A.C.] In addition to topics that may be chosen by the facility administrator, the preservice orientation must cover:
[Rule 59A-36.011(2)(d)1., F.A.C.] Resident’s rights; and,
[Rule 59A-36.011(2)(d)2., F.A.C.] The facility’s license type and services offered by the facility.
