[Rule 59A-36.004(2), F.A.C.] CHANGE IN USE OF SPACE REQUIRING AGENCY CENTRAL OFFICE APPROVAL. A change in the use of space that increases or decreases a facility’s capacity must not be made without prior approval from the Agency Central Office. Approval must be based on the compliance with the physical plant standards provided in Rule 59A-36.014, F.A.C., as well as documentation of compliance with applicable fire safety and sanitation inspection requirements referenced in Rule 59A-36.005, F.A.C.
