# Section 429.19(3), F.S.
Section: 429.19 Violations; imposition of administrative fines; grounds
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.19.html

[Section 429.19(3), F.S.] For purposes of this section, in determining if a penalty is to be imposed and in fixing the amount of the fine, the agency shall consider the following factors:
[Section 429.19(3)(a), F.S.] The gravity of the violation, including the probability that death or serious physical or emotional harm to a resident will result or has resulted, the severity of the action or potential harm, and the extent to which the provisions of the applicable laws or rules were violated.
[Section 429.19(3)(b), F.S.] Actions taken by the owner or administrator to correct violations.
[Section 429.19(3)(c), F.S.] Any previous violations.
[Section 429.19(3)(d), F.S.] The financial benefit to the facility of committing or continuing the violation.
[Section 429.19(3)(e), F.S.] The licensed capacity of the facility.
