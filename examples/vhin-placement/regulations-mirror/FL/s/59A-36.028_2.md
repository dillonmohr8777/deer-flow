# Rule 59A-36.028(2), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.028 ALF Minimum Core Training Curriculum Requirements
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.028

[Rule 59A-36.028(2), F.A.C.] MONITORING. The Agency reserves the right to do the following:
[Rule 59A-36.028(2)(a), F.A.C.] Require submission of the core training schedule from a core trainer;
[Rule 59A-36.028(2)(b), F.A.C.] Attend and monitor core training courses;
[Rule 59A-36.028(2)(c), F.A.C.] Review provider records and course materials pursuant to this rule; and,
[Rule 59A-36.028(2)(d), F.A.C.] Conduct on-site or virtual monitoring, follow-up monitoring, and require implementation of a corrective action plan if the provider does not adhere to the approved curriculum.
