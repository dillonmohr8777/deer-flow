# Section 429.12(1), F.S.
Section: 429.12 Sale or transfer of ownership of a facility
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.12.html

[Section 429.12(1), F.S.] The transferee shall notify the residents, in writing, of the change of ownership within 7 days after receipt of the new license.
