# Rule 59A-36.002(29), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.002 Definitions
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.002

[Rule 59A-36.002(29), F.A.C.] “Optional State Supplementation (OSS)” means the state program providing monthly payments to eligible residents pursuant to Section 409.212, F.S., and rule Chapter 65A-2, F.A.C.
