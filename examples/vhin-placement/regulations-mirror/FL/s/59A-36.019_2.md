# Rule 59A-36.019(2), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.019 Emergency Management
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.019

[Rule 59A-36.019(2), F.A.C.] EMERGENCY PLAN APPROVAL.
[Rule 59A-36.019(2)(a), F.A.C.] The facility must review and submit its emergency management plan on an annual basis in accordance with section 408.821(1), F.S.
[Rule 59A-36.019(2)(a)1., F.A.C.] A significant modification to a previously approved plan must be submitted within 30 days after the change. For the purposes of this rule, “significant modification” means a change to the information provided in support of the minimum required plan criteria, procedures, memorandums of understanding, contracts, or agreements identified in the plan, or appendices that alters the execution of the plan and the required arrangements made therein. Changes in spelling or grammar are not considered significant modifications for the purposes of this rule.
[Rule 59A-36.019(2)(a)2., F.A.C.] Changes in the name, address, phone number, email address or position of staff identified in the plan are not considered significant modifications for the purposes of this rule. Changes to that information must be submitted to the county emergency management agency as part of the emergency management plan submitted annually.
[Rule 59A-36.019(2)(a)3., F.A.C.] If a change to the emergency management plan is required to be submitted due to a significant modification, the change must be identified and described.
[Rule 59A-36.019(2)(a)4., F.A.C.] A change to the emergency management plan due to a significant modification does not alter the annual review date unless the change is due to a change of ownership of the facility.
[Rule 59A-36.019(2)(b), F.A.C.] The county emergency management agency is the final administrative authority for emergency management plans prepared by assisted living facilities.
[Rule 59A-36.019(2)(c), F.A.C.] Any plan approved by the county emergency management agency is considered to have met all the criteria and conditions established in this rule.
