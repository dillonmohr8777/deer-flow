# Rule 59A-36.006(4), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.006 Admission Procedures, Appropriateness of Placement and Continued Residency Criteria
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.006

[Rule 59A-36.006(4), F.A.C.] CONTINUED RESIDENCY. Except as follows in paragraphs (a) through (c) of this subsection, criteria for continued residency in any licensed facility must be the same as the criteria for admission. As part of the continued residency criteria, a resident must have a face-to-face medical examination by a health care practitioner at least every 3 years after the initial assessment, or after a significant change, whichever comes first. A significant change is defined in Rule 59A-36.002, F.A.C. The results of the examination must be recorded on the practitioner’s form or on AHCA Form 1823, which is incorporated by reference in paragraph (2)(b) of this rule and must be completed in accordance with that paragraph. Exceptions to the requirement to meet the criteria for continued residency are:
[Rule 59A-36.006(4)(a), F.A.C.] The resident may be bedridden for no more than 7 consecutive days, unless the resident is receiving licensed hospice services pursuant to Section 429.26(1)(c), F.S.
[Rule 59A-36.006(4)(b), F.A.C.] A resident requiring care of a stage 2 pressure sore may be retained provided that:
[Rule 59A-36.006(4)(b)1., F.A.C.] The resident contracts directly with a licensed home health agency or a nurse to provide care, or the facility has a limited nursing services license and services are provided pursuant to a plan of care issued by a health care practitioner,
[Rule 59A-36.006(4)(b)2., F.A.C.] The condition is documented in the resident’s record; and,
[Rule 59A-36.006(4)(b)3., F.A.C.] If the resident’s condition fails to improve within 30 days, as documented by a health care practitioner, the resident must be discharged from the facility.
[Rule 59A-36.006(4)(c), F.A.C.] A terminally ill resident who no longer meets the criteria for continued residency may continue to reside in the facility if the following conditions are met:
[Rule 59A-36.006(4)(c)1., F.A.C.] The resident qualifies for, is admitted to, and consents to receive services from a licensed hospice that coordinates and ensures the provision of any additional care and services that the resident may need;
[Rule 59A-36.006(4)(c)2., F.A.C.] Both the resident, or the resident’s legal representative if applicable, and the facility agree to continued residency;
[Rule 59A-36.006(4)(c)3., F.A.C.] A licensed hospice, in consultation with the facility, develops and implements an interdisciplinary care plan that specifies the services being provided by hospice and those being provided by the facility; and,
[Rule 59A-36.006(4)(c)4., F.A.C.] Documentation of the requirements of this paragraph is maintained in the resident’s file.
[Rule 59A-36.006(4)(d), F.A.C.] The facility administrator is responsible for monitoring the continued appropriateness of placement of a resident in the facility at all times.
