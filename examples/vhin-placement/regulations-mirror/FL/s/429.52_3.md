# Section 429.52(3), F.S.
Section: 429.52 Staff training and educational requirements
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.52.html

[Section 429.52(3), F.S.] The agency, in conjunction with providers, shall develop core training requirements for administrators consisting of core training learning objectives, a competency test, and a minimum required score to indicate successful passage of the core competency test. The required core competency test must cover at least the following topics:
[Section 429.52(3)(a), F.S.] State law and rules relating to assisted living facilities.
[Section 429.52(3)(b), F.S.] Resident rights and identifying and reporting abuse, neglect, and exploitation.
[Section 429.52(3)(c), F.S.] Special needs of elderly persons, persons with mental illness, and persons with developmental disabilities and how to meet those needs.
[Section 429.52(3)(d), F.S.] Nutrition and food service, including acceptable sanitation practices for preparing, storing, and serving food.
[Section 429.52(3)(e), F.S.] Medication management, recordkeeping, and proper techniques for assisting residents with self-administered medication.
[Section 429.52(3)(f), F.S.] Firesafety requirements, including fire evacuation drill procedures and other emergency procedures.
[Section 429.52(3)(g), F.S.] Care of persons with Alzheimer’s disease and related disorders.
