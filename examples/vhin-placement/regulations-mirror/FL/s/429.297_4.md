# Section 429.297(4), F.S.
Section: 429.297 Punitive damages; pleading; burden of proof
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.297.html

[Section 429.297(4), F.S.] The plaintiff must establish at trial, by clear and convincing evidence, its entitlement to an award of punitive damages. The “greater weight of the evidence” burden of proof applies to a determination of the amount of damages.
