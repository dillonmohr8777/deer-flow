# Rule 59A-36.015(3), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.015 Records
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.015

[Rule 59A-36.015(3), F.A.C.] RESIDENT RECORDS. Resident records must be maintained on the premises and include:
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(a), F.A.C.] Resident demographic data as follows:
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(a)1., F.A.C.] Name,
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(a)2., F.A.C.] Sex,
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(a)3., F.A.C.] Race,
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(a)4., F.A.C.] Date of birth,
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(a)5., F.A.C.] Place of birth, if known,
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(a)6., F.A.C.] Social security number,
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(a)7., F.A.C.] Medicaid and/or Medicare number, or name of other health insurance carrier,
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(a)8., F.A.C.] Name, address, and telephone number of next of kin, legal representative, or individual designated by the resident for notification in case of an emergency; and,
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(a)9., F.A.C.] Name, address, and telephone number of the resident’s health care practioner and case manager, if applicable.
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(b), F.A.C.] A copy of the Resident Health Assessment form, AHCA Form 1823 or the health care practitioner’s medical examination form described in Rule 59A-36.006, F.A.C.
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(c), F.A.C.] Any orders for medications, nursing services, therapeutic diets, do not resuscitate orders, or other services to be provided, supervised, or implemented by the facility that require a health care provider’s order.
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(d), F.A.C.] Documentation of a resident’s refusal of a therapeutic diet pursuant to Rule 59A-36.012, F.A.C., if applicable.
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(e), F.A.C.] The resident care record described in paragraph 59A-36.007(1)(f), F.A.C.
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(f), F.A.C.] A weight record that is initiated on admission. Information may be taken from AHCA Form 1823 or the resident’s health assessment. Residents receiving assistance with the activities of daily living must have their weight recorded semi-annually. This subsection does not apply to residents who are receiving licensed hospice services when such residents, their representatives, or their physicians request in writing that weights not be taken.
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(g), F.A.C.] For facilities that will have unlicensed staff assisting the resident with the self-administration of medication, a copy of the written informed consent described in Rule 59A-36.006, F.A.C., if such consent is not included in the resident’s contract.
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(h), F.A.C.] For facilities that manage a pill organizer, assist with self-administration of medications or administer medications for a resident, copies of the required medication records maintained pursuant to Rule 59A-36.008, F.A.C.
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(i), F.A.C.] A copy of the resident’s contract with the facility, including any addendums to the contract as described in Rule 59A-36.018, F.A.C.
[Rule 59A-36.015(3)(j), F.A.C.] For a facility whose owner, administrator, staff, or representative thereof, serves as an attorney in fact for a resident, a copy of the monthly written statement of any transaction made on behalf of the resident as required in Section 429.27, F.S.
