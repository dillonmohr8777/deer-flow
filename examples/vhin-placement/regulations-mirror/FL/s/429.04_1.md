# Section 429.04(1), F.S.
Section: 429.04 Facilities to be licensed; exemptions
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.04.html

[Section 429.04(1), F.S.] For the administration of this part, facilities to be licensed by the agency shall include all assisted living facilities as defined in this part.
