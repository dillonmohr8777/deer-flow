# Section 429.297(3), F.S.
Section: 429.297 Punitive damages; pleading; burden of proof
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.297.html

[Section 429.297(3), F.S.] In the case of an employer, principal, corporation, or other legal entity, punitive damages may be imposed for the conduct of an employee or agent only if the conduct of the employee or agent meets the criteria specified in subsection (2) and:
[Section 429.297(3)(a), F.S.] The employer, principal, corporation, or other legal entity actively and knowingly participated in such conduct;
[Section 429.297(3)(b), F.S.] The officers, directors, or managers of the employer, principal, corporation, or other legal entity condoned, ratified, or consented to such conduct; or
[Section 429.297(3)(c), F.S.] The employer, principal, corporation, or other legal entity engaged in conduct that constituted gross negligence and that contributed to the loss, damages, or injury suffered by the claimant.
