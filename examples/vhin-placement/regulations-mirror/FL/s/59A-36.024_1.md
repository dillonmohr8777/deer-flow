# Rule 59A-36.024(1), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.024 Waivers
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.024

[Rule 59A-36.024(1), F.A.C.] Application Process.
[Rule 59A-36.024(1)(a), F.A.C.] Licensed assisted living facilities proposing a waiver under this statute must submit the request in writing. All requests must include the facility name and address, license number, administrator’s name and contact information for the requestor, or its attorney. Petitions for waiver of rules other than for the objectives detailed in Section 429.41(4), F.S., including emergency waivers, will not be considered under this section but should follow the petition for waiver provisions of Section 120.542, F.S., and rule Chapter 28-104, Variance or Waiver, F.A.C.
[Rule 59A-36.024(1)(b), F.A.C.] The written request must address the elements required in Section 429.41(4), F.S. In addition, the following information must be included in order to demonstrate how a waiver of the stated rule will permit development of a concept that will achieve the purpose of the underlying statute:
[Rule 59A-36.024(1)(b)1., F.A.C.] The rule or rules for which the waiver is requested.
[Rule 59A-36.024(1)(b)2., F.A.C.] The licensee’s anticipated date or dates for implementation of the concept.
[Rule 59A-36.024(1)(b)3., F.A.C.] If applying based on cost-effectiveness or cost-savings, a cost-benefit analysis of the proposed alternative to both residents or potential residents as well as facility operations.
[Rule 59A-36.024(1)(b)4., F.A.C.] An analysis of the impact the alternative will have on the relevant local community, including any barriers such as zoning or use issues, which may need resolution prior to implementation.
[Rule 59A-36.024(1)(b)5., F.A.C.] Specific performance measures with an annual projection of objectives and goals to be achieved broken into quarterly increments or an annual projection of outcome measures, if the concept will be implemented in less than 90 days.
[Rule 59A-36.024(1)(b)6., F.A.C.] If applying based on cost-effectiveness or cost-savings, an annual budget projection for the proposed alternative broken into quarterly increments.
[Rule 59A-36.024(1)(c), F.A.C.] A waiver can be requested at the time of the initial license application, relicensure, or any time during the licensure period.
[Rule 59A-36.024(1)(d), F.A.C.] Waiver requests must be submitted to the Agency for Health Care Administration, Assisted Living Unit, 2727 Mahan Drive, Mail Stop 30, Tallahassee, Florida 32308-5403.
