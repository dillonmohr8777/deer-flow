# Section 429.275(1), F.S.
Section: 429.275 Business practice; personnel records; liability insurance
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.275.html

[Section 429.275(1), F.S.] The administrator or owner of a facility shall maintain accurate business records that identify, summarize, and classify funds received and expenses disbursed and shall use written accounting procedures and a recognized accounting system.
