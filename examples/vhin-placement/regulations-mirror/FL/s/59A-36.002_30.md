# Rule 59A-36.002(30), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.002 Definitions
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.002

[Rule 59A-36.002(30), F.A.C.] “Owner” means a person, partnership, association, limited liability company, or corporation, that owns or leases the facility that is licensed by the agency. The term does not include a person, partnership, association, limited liability company, or corporation that contracts only to manage or operate the facility.
