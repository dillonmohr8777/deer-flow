# Rule 59A-36.011(12), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.011 Staff Training Requirements and Competency Test
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.011

[Rule 59A-36.011(12), F.A.C.] TRAINING DOCUMENTATION AND MONITORING.
[Rule 59A-36.011(12)(a), F.A.C.] Except as otherwise noted, certificates, or copies of certificates, of any training required by this rule must be documented in the facility’s personnel files. The documentation must include the following:
[Rule 59A-36.011(12)(a)1., F.A.C.] The title of the training program,
[Rule 59A-36.011(12)(a)2., F.A.C.] The subject matter of the training program,
[Rule 59A-36.011(12)(a)3., F.A.C.] The training program agenda,
[Rule 59A-36.011(12)(a)4., F.A.C.] The number of hours of the training program,
[Rule 59A-36.011(12)(a)5., F.A.C.] The trainee’s name, dates of participation, and location of the training program,
[Rule 59A-36.011(12)(a)6., F.A.C.] The training provider’s name, dated signature and credentials, and professional license number, if applicable.
[Rule 59A-36.011(12)(b), F.A.C.] Upon successful completion of training pursuant to this rule, the training provider must issue a certificate to the trainee as specified in this rule.
[Rule 59A-36.011(12)(c), F.A.C.] The facility must provide the Department of Elder Affairs and the Agency for Health Care Administration with training documentation and training certificates for review, as requested. The department and agency reserve the right to attend and monitor all facility in-service training, which is intended to meet regulatory requirements.
