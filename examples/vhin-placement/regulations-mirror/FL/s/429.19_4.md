# Section 429.19(4), F.S.
Section: 429.19 Violations; imposition of administrative fines; grounds
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.19.html

[Section 429.19(4), F.S.] Each day of continuing violation after the date fixed for termination of the violation, as ordered by the agency, constitutes an additional, separate, and distinct violation.
