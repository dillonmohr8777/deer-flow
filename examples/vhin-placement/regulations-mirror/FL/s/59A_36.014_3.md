# Rule 59A-36.014(3), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.014 Physical Plant Standards
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.014

[Rule 59A-36.014(3), F.A.C.] OTHER REQUIREMENTS.
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(a), F.A.C.] All facilities must:
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(a)1., F.A.C.] Provide a safe living environment pursuant to Section 429.28(1)(a), F.S.;
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(a)2., F.A.C.] Be maintained free of hazards; and,
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(a)3., F.A.C.] Ensure that all existing architectural, mechanical, electrical and structural systems, and appurtenances are maintained in good working order.
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(b), F.A.C.] Pursuant to Section 429.27, F.S., residents must be given the option of using their own belongings as space permits. When the facility supplies the furnishings, each resident bedroom or sleeping area must have at least the following furnishings:
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(b)1., F.A.C.] A clean, comfortable bed with a mattress no less than 36 inches wide and 72 inches long, with the top surface of the mattress at a comfortable height to ensure easy access by the resident,
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(b)2., F.A.C.] A closet or wardrobe space for hanging clothes,
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(b)3., F.A.C.] A dresser, chest or other furniture designed for storage of clothing or personal effects,
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(b)4., F.A.C.] A table or nightstand, bedside lamp or floor lamp, and waste basket; and,
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(b)5., F.A.C.] A comfortable chair, if requested.
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(c), F.A.C.] The facility must maintain master or duplicate keys to resident bedrooms to be used in the event of an emergency.
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(d), F.A.C.] Residents who use portable bedside commodes must be provided with privacy during use.
[Rule 59A-36.014(3)(e), F.A.C.] Facilities must make available linens and personal laundry services for residents who require such services. Linens provided by a facility must be free of tears, stains and must not be threadbare.
