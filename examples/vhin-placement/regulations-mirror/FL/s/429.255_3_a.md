# Section 429.255(3)(a), F.S.
Section: 429.255 Use of personnel; emergency care
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.255.html

[Section 429.255(3)(a), F.S.] An assisted living facility licensed under this part with 17 or more beds shall have on the premises at all times a functioning automated external defibrillator as defined in s. 768.1325(2)(b).
[Section 429.255(3)(b), F.S.] The facility is encouraged to register the location of each automated external defibrillator with a local emergency medical services medical director.
[Section 429.255(3)(c), F.S.] The provisions of ss. 768.13 and 768.1325 apply to automated external defibrillators within the facility.
