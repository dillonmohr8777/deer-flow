# Section 429.53(1), F.S.
Section: 429.53 Consultation by the agency
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.53.html

[Section 429.53(1), F.S.] The area offices of licensure and certification of the agency shall provide consultation to the following upon request:
[Section 429.53(1)(a), F.S.] A licensee of a facility.
[Section 429.53(1)(b), F.S.] A person interested in obtaining a license to operate a facility under this part.
