# Section 429.17(6), F.S.
Section: 429.17 Expiration of license; renewal; conditional license
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.17.html

[Section 429.17(6), F.S.] The agency may by rule establish renewal procedures, identify forms, and specify documentation necessary to administer this section and may adopt rules to administer part II of chapter 408.
