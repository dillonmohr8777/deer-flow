# Rule 59A-36.007(1), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.007 Resident Care Standards
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.007

[Rule 59A-36.007(1), F.A.C.] SUPERVISION. Facilities must offer personal supervision as appropriate for each resident, including the following:
[Rule 59A-36.007(1)(a), F.A.C.] Monitoring of the quantity and quality of resident diets in accordance with Rule 59A-36.012, F.A.C.
[Rule 59A-36.007(1)(b), F.A.C.] Daily observation by designated staff of the activities of the resident while on the premises, and awareness of the general health, safety, and physical and emotional well-being of the resident.
[Rule 59A-36.007(1)(c), F.A.C.] Maintaining a general awareness of the resident’s whereabouts. The resident may travel independently in the community.
[Rule 59A-36.007(1)(d), F.A.C.] Contacting the resident’s health care provider and other appropriate party such as the resident’s family, guardian, health care surrogate, or case manager if the resident exhibits a significant change.
[Rule 59A-36.007(1)(e), F.A.C.] Contacting the resident’s family, guardian, health care surrogate, or case manager if the resident is discharged or moves out.
[Rule 59A-36.007(1)(f), F.A.C.] Maintaining a written record, updated as needed, of any significant changes, any illnesses that resulted in medical attention, changes in the method of medication administration, or other changes that resulted in the provision of additional services.
