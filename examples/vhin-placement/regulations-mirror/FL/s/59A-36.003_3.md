# Rule 59A-36.003(3), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.003 Licensing and Change of Ownership
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.003

[Rule 59A-36.003(3), F.A.C.] CONDITIONAL LICENSE. Except as provided in Section 429.14, F.S., the agency may issue a conditional license to a facility if, at the time of license renewal the facility is found to have uncorrected violations that the facility has had an opportunity to correct. The issuance of a conditional license does not change the biennial license expiration date.
