[Rule 59A-36.006(1)(a)11., F.A.C.] Residents admitted to standard, limited nursing services, or limited mental health licensed facilities may not require any of the following nursing services:
[Rule 59A-36.006(1)(a)11.a., F.A.C.] Artificial airway management of any kind, except that of continuous positive airway pressure may be provided through the use of a CPAP or bipap machine;
[Rule 59A-36.006(1)(a)11.b., F.A.C.] Assistance with tube feeding,
[Rule 59A-36.006(1)(a)11.c., F.A.C.] Monitoring of blood gases,
[Rule 59A-36.006(1)(a)11.d., F.A.C.] Management of post-surgical drainage tubes and wound vacuum devices;
[Rule 59A-36.006(1)(a)11.e., F.A.C.] The administration of blood products in the facility; or
[Rule 59A-36.006(1)(a)11.f., F.A.C.] Treatment of surgical incisions or wounds, unless the surgical incision or wound and the underlying condition have been stabilized and a plan of care has been developed. The plan of care must be maintained in the resident’s record.
