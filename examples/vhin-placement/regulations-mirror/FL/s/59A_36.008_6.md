# Rule 59A-36.008(6), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.008 Medication Practices
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.008

[Rule 59A-36.008(6), F.A.C.] MEDICATION STORAGE AND DISPOSAL.
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(a), F.A.C.] In order to accommodate the needs and preferences of residents and to encourage residents to remain as independent as possible, residents may keep their medications, both prescription and over-the-counter, in their possession both on or off the facility premises. Residents may also store their medication in their rooms or apartments if either the room is kept locked when residents are absent or the medication is stored in a secure place that is out of sight of other residents.
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(b), F.A.C.] Both prescription and over-the-counter medications for residents must be centrally stored if:
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(b)1., F.A.C.] The facility administers the medication;
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(b)2., F.A.C.] The resident requests central storage. The facility must maintain a list of all medications being stored pursuant to such a request;
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(b)3., F.A.C.] The medication is determined and documented by the health care provider to be hazardous if kept in the personal possession of the person for whom it is prescribed;
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(b)4., F.A.C.] The resident fails to maintain the medication in a safe manner as described in this paragraph;
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(b)5., F.A.C.] The facility determines that, because of physical arrangements and the conditions or habits of residents, the personal possession of medication by a resident poses a safety hazard to other residents, or
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(b)6., F.A.C.] The facility’s rules and regulations require central storage of medication and that policy has been provided to the resident before admission as required in Rule 59A-36.006, F.A.C.
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(c), F.A.C.] Centrally stored medications must be:
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(c)1., F.A.C.] Kept in a locked cabinet; locked cart; or other locked storage receptacle, room, or area at all times;
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(c)2., F.A.C.] Located in an area free of dampness and abnormal temperature, except that a medication requiring refrigeration must be kept refrigerated. Refrigerated medications must be secured by being kept in a locked container within the refrigerator, by keeping the refrigerator locked, or by keeping the area in which the refrigerator is located locked;
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(c)3., F.A.C.] Accessible to staff responsible for filling pill-organizers, assisting with self-administration of medication, or administering medication. Such staff must have ready access to keys or codes to the medication storage areas at all times; and,
[Rule 59A-36.008(6)(c)4., F.A.C.] Kept separately from the medications of other residents and properly closed or sealed.
