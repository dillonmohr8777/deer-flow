# Rule 59A-36.024(3), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.024 Waivers
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.024

[Rule 59A-36.024(3), F.A.C.] The agency, in consultation with the department, will evaluate all requests in light of the likelihood the concept, as described in detail, will achieve the underlying statutory objectives of innovative or cost effective congregate care alternatives to enable individuals to age in place, as provided in Section 429.41(4), F.S. Waivers may be granted only so long as there is reasonable assurance that the health, safety or welfare of residents will not be endangered by the waiver.
