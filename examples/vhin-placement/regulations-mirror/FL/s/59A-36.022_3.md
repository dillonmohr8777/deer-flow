# Rule 59A-36.022(3), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.022 Limited Nursing Services
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.022

[Rule 59A-36.022(3), F.A.C.] RECORDS.
[Rule 59A-36.022(3)(a), F.A.C.] A record of all residents receiving limited nursing services and the type of services provided must be maintained at the facility.
[Rule 59A-36.022(3)(b), F.A.C.] Nursing progress notes must be maintained for each resident who receives limited nursing services from facility staff.
[Rule 59A-36.022(3)(c), F.A.C.] A nursing assessment conducted at least monthly must be maintained on each resident who receives a limited nursing service.
