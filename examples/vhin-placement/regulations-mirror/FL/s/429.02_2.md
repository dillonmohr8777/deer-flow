# Section 429.02(2), F.S.
Section: 429.02 Definitions
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.02.html

[Section 429.02(2), F.S.] “Administrator” means an individual at least 21 years of age who is responsible for the operation and maintenance of an assisted living facility.
