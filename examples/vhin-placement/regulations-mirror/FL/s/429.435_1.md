# Section 429.435(1), F.S.
Section: 429.435 Uniform firesafety standards
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.435.html

[Section 429.435(1), F.S.] EVACUATION CAPABILITY.—A firesafety evacuation capability determination shall be conducted within 6 months after the date of initial licensure of an assisted living facility, if required.
