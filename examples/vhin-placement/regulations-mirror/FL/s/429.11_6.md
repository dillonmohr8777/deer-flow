# Section 429.11(6), F.S.
Section: 429.11 Initial application for license
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.11.html

[Section 429.11(6), F.S.] A county or municipality may not issue a business tax receipt that is being obtained for the purpose of operating a facility regulated under this part without first ascertaining that the applicant has been licensed to operate such facility at the specified location or locations by the agency. The agency shall furnish to local agencies responsible for issuing business tax receipts sufficient instruction for making such determinations.
