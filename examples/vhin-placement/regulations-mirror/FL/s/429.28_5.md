# Section 429.28(5), F.S.
Section: 429.28 Resident bill of rights
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.28.html

[Section 429.28(5), F.S.] A facility or employee of a facility may not serve notice upon a resident to leave the premises or take any other retaliatory action against any person who:
[Section 429.28(5)(a), F.S.] Exercises any right set forth in this section.
[Section 429.28(5)(b), F.S.] Appears as a witness in any hearing, inside or outside the facility.
[Section 429.28(5)(c), F.S.] Files a civil action alleging a violation of the provisions of this part or notifies a state attorney or the Attorney General of a possible violation of such provisions.
