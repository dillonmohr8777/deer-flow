# Rule 59A-36.021(8), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.021 Extended Congregate Care Services
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.021

[Rule 59A-36.021(8), F.A.C.] RECORDS. In addition to the records required in Rule 59A-36.015, F.A.C., a facility providing extended congregate care services must maintain the following:
[Rule 59A-36.021(8)(a), F.A.C.] The service plans for each resident receiving extended congregate care services;
[Rule 59A-36.021(8)(b), F.A.C.] The nursing progress notes for each resident receiving nursing services from the facility’s staff;
[Rule 59A-36.021(8)(c), F.A.C.] Nursing assessments; and,
[Rule 59A-36.021(8)(d), F.A.C.] The facility’s extended congregate care policies and procedures.
