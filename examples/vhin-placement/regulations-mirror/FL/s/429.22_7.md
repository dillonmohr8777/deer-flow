# Section 429.22(7), F.S.
Section: 429.22 Receivership proceedings
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.22.html

[Section 429.22(7), F.S.] A receiver may be held liable in a personal capacity only for the receiver’s own gross negligence, intentional acts, or breach of fiduciary duty.
