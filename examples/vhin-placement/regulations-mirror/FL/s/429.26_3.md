# Section 429.26(3), F.S.
Section: 429.26 Appropriateness of placements; examinations of residents
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.26.html

[Section 429.26(3), F.S.] A physician, physician assistant, or advanced practice registered nurse who is employed by an assisted living facility to provide an initial examination for admission purposes may not have financial interests in the facility.
