# Section 429.256(5), F.S.
Section: 429.256 Assistance with self-administration of medication and with other tasks
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.256.html

[Section 429.256(5), F.S.] Assistance with the self-administration of medication by an unlicensed person as described in this section shall not be considered administration as defined in s. 465.003.
