# Rule 59A-36.002(37), F.A.C.
Section: 59A-36.002 Definitions
Official: https://www.flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=59A-36.002

[Rule 59A-36.002(37), F.A.C.] “Staff in Regular Contact” or “Staff in Direct Contact” mean all staff whose duties may require them to interact with residents on a daily basis.
