# Section 429.55(1), F.S.
Section: 429.55 Consumer information
Official: http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0429/Sections/0429.55.html

[Section 429.55(1), F.S.] CONSUMER INFORMATION WEBSITE.—The Legislature finds that consumers need additional information on the quality of care and service in assisted living facilities in order to select the best facility for themselves or their loved ones. Therefore, the Agency for Health Care Administration shall create content that is easily accessible through the home page of the agency’s website either directly or indirectly through links to one or more other established websites of the agency’s choosing. The website must be searchable by facility name, license type, city, or zip code. The agency shall include all content in its possession on the website and add content when received from facilities. At a minimum, the content must include:
[Section 429.55(1)(a), F.S.] Information on each licensed assisted living facility, including, but not limited to:
[Section 429.55(1)(a)1., F.S.] The name and address of the facility.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)2., F.S.] The name of the owner or operator of the facility.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)3., F.S.] The number and type of licensed beds in the facility.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)4., F.S.] The types of licenses held by the facility.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)5., F.S.] The facility’s license expiration date and status.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)6., F.S.] The total number of clients that the facility is licensed to serve and the most recently available occupancy levels.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)7., F.S.] The number of private and semiprivate rooms offered.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)8., F.S.] The bed-hold policy.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)9., F.S.] The religious affiliation, if any, of the assisted living facility.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)10., F.S.] The languages spoken by the staff.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)11., F.S.] Availability of nurses.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)12., F.S.] Forms of payment accepted, including, but not limited to, Medicaid, Medicaid long-term managed care, private insurance, health maintenance organization, United States Department of Veterans Affairs, CHAMPUS program, or workers’ compensation coverage.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)13., F.S.] Indication if the licensee is operating under bankruptcy protection.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)14., F.S.] Recreational and other programs available.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)15., F.S.] Special care units or programs offered.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)16., F.S.] Whether the facility is a part of a retirement community that offers other services pursuant to this part or part III of this chapter, part II or part III of chapter 400, or chapter 651.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)17., F.S.] Links to the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program website and the program’s statewide toll-free telephone number.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)18., F.S.] Links to the websites of the providers.
[Section 429.55(1)(a)19., F.S.] Other relevant information that the agency currently collects.
